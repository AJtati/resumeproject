# Portfolio surface

## Scope and mode
One interactive React portfolio for Ajith Surya Thati. Mode: Experience.

## Visitor and task
Recruiters, hiring teams, and engineering peers explore the work history, capabilities, projects, and education, then contact Ajith. The complete resume stays available; interaction adds context and a sense of discovery.

## Proof and constraints
Use the supplied resume, an illustrated portrait based on the supplied photo, the EKS blueprint from the resume, and the ten projects in Ajith's listed repositories. Distinguish hosted web apps from desktop and mobile app builds. Link only to verified live websites and publicly accessible repositories; do not expose private repository links. Do not invent business outcomes or metrics. Sound control starts on by default and visitors can turn it off; browser audio starts after a user gesture. Contact remains visible on every screen. The page must adapt to mobile, keyboard navigation, and reduced-motion settings. Draw from Apple-like restraint and expressive motion resources without copying a specific site.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A bright editorial portfolio makes a real engineering career feel like a story visitors can enter and explore. It refuses both the undifferentiated CV and dark instrument-panel cliches.

OWN-WORLD: Near-white surfaces, near-black system typography, a confident blue action color, and soft sky and peach fields form the light visual world. Let generous space and a large illustrated portrait carry the opening. The character subtly leans toward pointer movement and responds to scroll; a soft cursor-following light field adds depth behind the hero on fine-pointer devices. Both effects honor reduced-motion preferences and stay off touch-only devices. Use short, interruptible transitions elsewhere. Sound effects are short confirmations on navigation and selection, never a loop.

STORY: The visitor enters through Ajith's portrait and role, then chooses Work, Career, Toolkit, or Profile. Project cases and job records reveal all supplied resume details. Contact stays one action away.

FIRST VIEWPORT: At desktop width, a light translucent header sits above a generous two-column opening. The left column sets Ajith's name on exactly two lines, followed by his role, one-sentence summary, and a blue Explore My Work action. A transparent illustrated portrait occupies a soft blue field at right and reacts to the pointer. A quiet section navigation and EKS project link ground the bottom of the page. Keep the phone number in Profile; show a concise Call action in the header.

FORM: Light, product-storytelling portfolio. Preserve the portrait-led chapter structure while simplifying the color and ornament and making the portrait responsive to interaction.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
