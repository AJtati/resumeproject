# Portfolio surface

## Scope and mode
One interactive React portfolio for Ajith Surya Thati. Mode: Experience.

## Visitor and task
Recruiters, hiring teams, and engineering peers explore the work history, capabilities, projects, and education, then contact Ajith. The complete resume stays available; interaction adds context and a sense of discovery.

## Proof and constraints
Use the supplied resume, real profile photo, and four named projects. Do not invent business outcomes or metrics. Sound is opt-in and off at first visit. Contact remains visible on every screen. The page must adapt to mobile, keyboard navigation, and reduced-motion settings. Inspired by the referenced site's explicit sound-enabled invitation, while retaining an original visual identity and interaction design.

OWN-WORLD: Midnight blue-green surfaces, warm chalk typography, amber active channels, mint/cyan signal traces, crisp coordinate marks, and shaped instrument controls. Use the comp-ranked Noto Sans Display for display/navigation, IBM Plex Sans for reading, and IBM Plex Mono for metadata. Sound effects are small synthesized signal confirmations, available only after an explicit sound-on action. Raise — Teletext: chapter indices are stable and visible.
