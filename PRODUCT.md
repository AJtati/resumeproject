# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring teams evaluating Ajith Surya Thati for platform/DevOps engineering roles, and engineering peers exploring his projects. Both should be able to understand his work and contact him.

## Product Purpose

Ajith's professional portfolio presents his work history, engineering skills, education, and software projects. Success means visitors can explore his experience, see concrete evidence of how he works, and get in touch.

## Positioning

The portfolio brings production platform engineering experience together with independently built desktop, cloud, and image-processing applications.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from professional profiles or applications and explore the portfolio on desktop or mobile. It is a React single-page application with public professional contact links.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the full factual resume content supplied in `Resume.pdf` and portfolio project details; do not invent metrics or results.
- Current project stack is React 19 with Framer Motion and React Icons.
- Sound defaults on in the interface, and visitors can turn it off; audible effects begin after the first user interaction to satisfy browser audio policies.
- Visitors should be able to reach contact information without completing an interaction.

## Evidence on Hand

- Resume source: `/Users/ajithsuryathati/Downloads/Resume.pdf` (user supplied); its content includes work history, skills, education, and projects.
- Existing portrait asset: `src/assets/profile.jpg`.
- Project list: the EKS platform blueprint from the resume plus the ten additional projects the user identified in their repositories. Public web links and repository visibility were checked before adding links; app-only projects are labeled as such.
- No project screenshots, customer testimonials, or independently verified impact metrics are supplied.

## Product Principles

- Show the actual work and explain it with specific, verifiable details.
- Make exploration useful without hiding essential resume information behind interaction.
- Keep contact direct and easy to find.
- Respect visitor control over sound and motion.
